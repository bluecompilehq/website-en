Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information Blue Compile collects through this website, why, and what rights you have.

What we collect

When you fill in the contact form, we receive the details you enter: name, email, phone (if you give it) and your message. Giving us this information is voluntary, but without a name and email we can't get back to you.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, understand your project and send you a quote. We won't send you marketing emails without your explicit consent, and we don't sell or hand your details to others for their own use.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Messages are kept in our email inbox and with the service providers that run this website and its form for us. We only share information with anyone else if the law requires it.

Cookies and third-party services

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may receive basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to run and secure their service.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it or delete it. To do so, email us.
